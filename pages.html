Privacy

This policy is for this sbm Sync server, the sbm app for Android and the sbm add-on for Firefox and Chrome.

What the server keeps, and why

  • Your email address and a bcrypt hash of your password, never the password itself: to sign you in. When this server checks email addresses, it also sends you an email with a link that confirms your address.
  • Your bookmark file, the last 50 versions that your devices sent: to sync your devices.
  • A hash of the sign-in token of each device: to know your devices.
  • When you subscribe: your Stripe customer ID and the state of your subscription: to bill you.

The server does not sell or share this data, and does not use it for ads. There are no analytics and no trackers. The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Data goes to and from the server over HTTPS. On the server it is not encrypted: the operator of the server can read it.

Payments

Stripe takes the payments. This server never sees your card. See the Stripe privacy policy.

Delete your data

Delete your account on the account page. The server deletes the account and all versions of your bookmark file at once.

The app and the add-on

They keep your bookmarks on your device. They send them only to the sync server that you sign in to, and only after you sign in. Without an account, no data leaves your device.
